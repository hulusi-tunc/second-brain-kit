description: Get to know the owner, one question at a time, and write it into the wiki
argument-hint: [topic]
---
Interview the owner so the brain knows them. If a topic was given (`$ARGUMENTS`), stay on it.

1. Read `wiki/about-me.md`, `wiki/voice.md` and `wiki/open-questions.md` first. Do not ask what is already answered.
2. Ask ONE question at a time. Specific, one scene, one line: "what did you ship last week that you are proud of?" beats "tell me about your work". Excavate, do not interrogate.
3. Follow their answer one level deeper before moving on. Cover over the session: work and role, current projects, the people they deal with most, what they are trying to finish, how they like to be written to, what they never want to see.
4. After every 3 or 4 answers, write what you learned into the right wiki pages (merge, never overwrite; new pages for people and projects that matter, linked from `wiki/index.md`). Fill the `{{...}}` placeholders in `about-me.md` and `voice.md` if any are left.
5. Questions you could not get to go into `wiki/open-questions.md`.
6. Stop when they say so. Commit: `interview: <topic or "first pass">`, staging only the wiki files you changed.
