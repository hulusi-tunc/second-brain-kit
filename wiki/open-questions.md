# Open questions

Things the brain does not know yet. The morning brief asks one a day. Answer by capturing (`brain "..."`); `/translate` or `/log` moves the answer into the right entry and strikes the line through here. Add the word Parked to a line to stop it being asked.

- What does a good week look like for you, concretely?
- Who are the five people you work with most, and what do you owe each of them right now?
- What are you trying to finish in the next three months?
