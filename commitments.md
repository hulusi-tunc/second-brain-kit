# Commitments

Who owes what to whom. The ledger behind `/today`, `/prep` and the morning brief. Rows are added by `/translate` (every next-steps item in a meeting note), `/log` (promises made in a session) and by hand. Status is updated whenever a later source shows the outcome; never add a duplicate row.

`ID` is `c` plus 7 hex characters and never changes. `brain done <id>` in a terminal marks a row done.

Status: `open`, `overdue` (past due, nothing seen), `check` (should be done, not verified), `done`, `slipped` (missed the date, still open), `dropped` (was never a real promise). Dates are YYYY-MM-DD. Source is the archive file, the channel, or a link.
